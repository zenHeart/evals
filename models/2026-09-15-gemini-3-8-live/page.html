Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking

September 15, 2026

Today, we are announcing Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, our most capable speech-to-speech models designed for real-time, low-latency, natural dialogue and complex agentic workflows.

Gemini 3.8 Live delivers instantaneous turn-taking, seamless mid-conversation switching across 97 languages, and asynchronous function calling. Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking introduces background reasoning during live audio streams, enabling the model to solve complex multi-step problems without breaking the flow of speech.
