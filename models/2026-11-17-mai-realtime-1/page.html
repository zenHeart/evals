MAI Realtime 1: Leading voice AI at $0.01 per audio minute

MAI Realtime 1, our first speech-to-speech model, is built for naturally flowing conversations for real-world enterprise applications starting at $0.01 per audio minute.1

Our model outperforms GPT Realtime 2.1 on quality, speed, and price across public benchmarks and Microsoft product evaluations. It sits on the performance-price Pareto frontier for both Big Bench Audio speech reasoning, and Tau-Voice agentic task completion benchmark.2

Natural conversations that get things done

Building a voice agent people actually want to talk to takes more than just a good voice. It takes the right mix of conversation, intelligence, and action working together simultaneously. MAI Realtime 1 brings that together through the following capabilities:

  • Natural conversation: MAI Realtime 1 manages interruptions, follows quick acknowledgments, and knows when to wait for someone to finish speaking
  • Adaptive intelligence: A fast, responsive system keeps the conversation flowing in real time. When a task requires deeper thinking, it taps into a more powerful reasoning engine delivering thoughtful answers without awkward pauses
  • Robust understanding: It recognizes requests even with background noise or other people talking
  • Real-world action: It takes on complex requests and connects to enterprise systems and tools, so voice agents can do more than simply answer questions
  • Adaptable personas: It adjusts tone, style, and behavior through prompting to suit different customers, domains, and use cases

MAI Realtime 1 is also compatible with the OpenAI Realtime API, making it straightforward for developers to test the model and migrate existing voice applications.

Ready for real-world applications

Companies including Agoda and Reservations.ai are already putting MAI Realtime 1 to the test.

We’re also bringing MAI Realtime 1 to Dynamics 365 Contact Center for customer service applications, and to Dragon Copilot, to support AI-simulated patient conversations that help nurses practice clinical interactions.

Get started

Try out MAI Realtime 1 in MAI Labs or register for the Foundry limited-access preview. Preview customers won’t be billed until public preview begins.

About these results

Results reflect specific evaluation configurations and workloads. Performance, latency, and cost comparisons vary by application and methodology.

  1. Pricing: Base speech-to-speech usage is priced at $0.01 per minute. Reasoning is billed separately at $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens.
  2. Competitive performance: MAI Realtime 1 was evaluated against GPT Realtime 2.1 using public benchmarks including Big Bench Audio and τ-Voice, alongside Dynamics 365 Contact Center evaluations.
